About ArcAssist-Web Bot

ArcAssist-Web Bot is an early prototype for evidence-grounded architectural decision preparation in Web-system evolution.

The implemented path combines a Web chat interface, a local architecture corpus, semantic retrieval, prompt orchestration, and an external LLM service. Its goal is to draft material for human review, not to autonomously accept architectural decisions.

This camera-ready artifact accompanies the WBOTS 2026 paper ArcAssist-Web Bot: A Prototype Instantiation for Evidence-Grounded Architectural Decision Support, by Vanusa Rodrigues de Oliveira, Bento Rafael Siqueira, and Samira Santos da Silva (Federal University of Lavras, Brazil).

The authors gratefully acknowledge financial support from FAPEMIG through projects CIN II APQ-06513-25 and CEX-BIS-00192-25.
